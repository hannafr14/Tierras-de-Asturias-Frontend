## Demonstration Walkthrough

1. Search for products and select an available category.
2. Open a product and its producer's page, including navigation from the map.
3. Add products to the cart and change quantities.
4. Complete a demonstration order as a guest.
5. Repeat with a registered account and view the order in `Mi cuenta`.
6. Log out and verify that private routes require sign-in.

Use a development database and fictitious data. No payment is taken.
